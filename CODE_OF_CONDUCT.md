# Code of conduct

Cybermaps contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique code and claims, not people.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to keep a change within the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information is not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns privately through [CYBER BRAND](https://cyberbrand.net/).
Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing Cybermaps.
